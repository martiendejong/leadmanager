# Our products (catalog used for lead fit)

Source: jengo-knowledge-private/sales/prospect-list-2026-08.md and the AI one-pagers. Keys are stable ids.

| key | name | for whom | what they get | typical trigger on the prospect's own site |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TRIP | Trip planner / AI tourism platform | hospitality, boat rental, hotels, attractions in tourist areas | multilingual trip planner, AI FAQ, Chinese payments (Alipay/WeChat), SEO | site only in Dutch while serving tourists, no online booking, no international payments |
| STUDIO | Studio / coach app | yoga, fitness, coaching, wellness studios | schedule, bookings, member management, AI coach, white label | booking by mail or phone only, manual membership admin |
| PROV | Provenance / registry services | dealers, galleries, auction houses, appraisers, collectors | registers, provenance reports, archive digitisation | value depends on documented history, collection or serial-number goods |
| WEB | Website + AI assistant package | small service businesses (trades, advisers, clinics, offices) | new site, RAG chat on own content, AI findability, workflow automation (EUR 1,700 + EUR 100/month reference) | dated site, no quote or intake form, many repeated customer questions |
| REALTOR | Realtor AI | estate agents | AI chat and property matching | estate agent without AI tools |
| AIMEDEW | AI employee | any small business with admin load | bookkeeping prep, admin, secretary, customer service, process automation; EUR 500/month full-time equivalent or EUR 2.50/hour, fixed price up front, human checked | owner does own bookkeeping, invoicing, planning, mail; one-person business |
| AIDEV | AI programming team | businesses needing software changes | tasks on a board with fixed price up front, EUR 125 or EUR 500 per month | needs a tool, integration or feature built |
| AIVIND | AI findability scan and offer | professional services (law, finance, advice) | scan of how AI search engines present the firm, plus offer | professional services firm whose site is thin or not structured for AI search |

Rules for fit statements: price per outcome, never per hour. A fit needs an observed trigger on the business's own pages (quote it). If nothing observable supports a product, write no fit; "no clear fit" is a valid answer.
